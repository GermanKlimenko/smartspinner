Hello [COMPANY] PCBA Team,

My name is German Klimenko. I am developing SmartSpinner v0.3, a handheld four-arm persistence-of-vision display with Bluetooth connectivity.

I would like to request a preliminary DFM review and budgetary quotation for 10 complete electronic sets. Each set is expected to include:

- 1 four-arm main PCB, approximately 96–100 mm overall diameter;
- 80 top-facing addressable RGB LEDs, 20 LEDs per arm;
- 4 small vertical tip PCBs;
- 12 addressable RGB LEDs on each tip PCB, 48 tip LEDs per set;
- nRF52840/BLE control;
- Hall sensor and IMU for angular synchronization;
- LiPo charging and 3.3 V / 5 V power circuitry;
- turnkey component sourcing, PCB fabrication, SMT assembly and AOI.

The requested lot therefore contains 10 main PCBs and 40 tip PCBs. Batteries and the 3D-printed enclosure should not be included in the PCBA shipment.
