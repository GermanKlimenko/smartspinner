# Send checklist

- Replace `[COMPANY]` in the email subject and greeting.
- Attach `SmartSpinner_v0_4_RFQ_Send_Package.zip` or provide its download link.
- Ask the supplier to acknowledge the `NOT FOR FABRICATION` status.
- Request separate prices for engineering and manufacturing.
- Request 5-set, 10-set and 25-set quantity breaks.
- Request one first article before the remaining build.
- Require updated editable KiCad sources as a contractual deliverable.
- Require zero-error DRC and final DFM reports before approval.
- Exclude LiPo batteries from the shipment.
- Do not authorize fabrication until technical questions and revised files have been reviewed.
