# SmartSpinner v0.4 - supplier RFQ package

## Purpose

This package is ready to send to a PCB engineering and turnkey PCBA supplier for:

1. review and completion of the supplied KiCad layout;
2. DFM, footprint and sourcing review;
3. a quotation for one first article followed by 10 complete electronic sets.

The current design is an engineering release and is **not approved for fabrication**. The supplier must return updated editable KiCad sources and a zero-error DRC report for customer approval before manufacturing.

## One complete electronic set

- 1 four-arm main PCB with 80 top RGB pixels;
- 4 identical vertical tip PCBs with 12 RGB pixels each;
- 128 RGB pixels total;
- nRF52840 BLE, BMI270 IMU and DRV5033 Hall sensor;
- protected 1S LiPo input, 3.3 V logic and switched 5 V LED rail;
- batteries and 3D-printed enclosure excluded from the PCBA shipment.
